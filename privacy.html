Privacy Policy for Elon Uploader

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Elon Uploader is a Google OAuth app operated by KaneKorp (UK) for The Muskverse Daily.

What the app does

The app is used only by the channel owner to upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

What data the app accesses

The app accesses only the channel owner's YouTube account data needed to upload and edit videos on that channel.

The app collects no data from viewers or any other users.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored securely and are not shared with or sold to anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google

Use of Google services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
